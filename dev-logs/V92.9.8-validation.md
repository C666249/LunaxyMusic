## Product/performance decisions

- V92.9.7's playlist-size dispatch to the separate ListView detail implementation is intentionally retired after the device crash report.
- Playlist detail now establishes header + skeleton geometry immediately, then constructs one real song row per frame in bounded batches.
- Real rows reveal through `CurtainRevealFrame` clip geometry from left to right; skeleton and content share final dimensions so replacement does not jump layout.
- Initial target is 10 rows; small prewarm reaches at most roughly 30 rows. Deeper materialization is driven by scroll proximity, 10 rows per batch.
- Tail loading is communicated by `FluidLoadingIconView`, not text.
- Heavy lateral root destinations and same-tab detail pushes can use a cancellable skeleton shell; the shell moves immediately, real construction starts after the navigation settles, and rapid retarget increments a generation token so obsolete deferred builds are discarded.
- Locate-current-song also materializes distant rows one per frame, and visible-row deletion collapses the enclosing curtain slot so progressive loading does not leave empty geometry.
- Search background/cancellation/stable-append behavior from V92.9.7 remains.
